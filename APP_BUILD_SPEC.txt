SMARTAGING FULL-LOOP DEMO APP SPEC

Purpose: pitch/demo prototype using synthetic data only. No clinical validation, diagnosis, treatment efficacy or medical-device claims.

LANDING:
- 100 synthetic participants | 12 months | 600 assessments | 4 domains
- Profile filter + Participant ID + Visit selector
- Permanent banner: “Synthetic demonstration cohort — not clinical validation or medical diagnosis.”

PATIENT VIEW:
1) Header: Participant, profile, visit.
2) Four domain cards:
   Camera/Function; Wearable/Physiology; Blood/Metabolic; Body Composition/BIA.
   Each card: issue score 0–100 (DEMO severity only), flag, baseline-to-current trend.
3) Overall demo score + flag.
   IMPORTANT: label “Demo prioritization score — not a validated clinical risk score.”
4) “What changed?”: show top 2 weighted domains.
5) “Recommended next actions”: use Recommended_action_1/2 from CSV.
6) “Re-measure”: show Follow_up.
7) Full-loop trajectory:
   Baseline -> Detect -> Recommend -> Re-measure -> Response.
8) Intervention Response profile:
   Month 6 marker = “AI-guided preventive action plan (SIMULATED)”.
   Compare Month 9/12 with Month 6. Say “improved/stabilized after simulated action plan”, never “AI caused improvement.”

WEIGHTS FOR DEMO PRIORITIZATION:
- Wearable/Physiology 30%
- Blood/Metabolic 30%
- Body Composition 25%
- Camera/Function 15%
These weights are product-design assumptions for the demo, NOT validated clinical weights.

AI STRATEGY:
- Detection/flagging = transparent longitudinal rules.
- Recommendation content = curated evidence-based action library + safety/escalation rules.
- Generative AI = explain/prioritize/personalize wording, not autonomous diagnosis or prescribing.

HERO DEMO:
Use an Early Change participant to show subtle asynchronous changes.
Then switch to Intervention Response to show Month 6 simulated plan and Month 9/12 follow-up.
